How the index is read

Readiness index = machine single-core ÷ your bar × 100. Single-core (Geekbench 7) is what daily office responsiveness rides on — most office apps don't spread across cores.

≥ 100 · Ready 80–99 · Aging < 80 · Below bar

Why the SSD is scored separately

Geekbench never touches storage, yet disk speed governs boot, app-launch and file-open snappiness. Measure random 4K read with fio — the same binary on both OSes (apt install fio / brew install fio).

> 30 MB/s · Fast SSD 8–30 · Basic SSD < 8 · Disk-bound

Thresholds are practical rules of thumb, not lab standards — random-4K numbers vary with queue depth, so keep the fio command identical on both machines. Geekbench 7 (July 2026) rebased the scale, so its scores are not comparable to Geekbench 6 — set your bar in GB7 and re-test both machines in GB7. GB7 scores stay comparable year to year until a future version rebases again. Example data below is illustrative, not a measurement of your machines.
